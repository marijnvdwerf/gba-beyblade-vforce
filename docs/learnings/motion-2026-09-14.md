# sub_805041C (0x0805041C, src/motion.c)

Motion-group integrator: position += velocity, velocity += acceleration, copy positions to the sprites. When `mode & 3` is set, one axis (`mode & 1` selects x) has a boundary at `group->unk10`: on crossing, the position clamps to the boundary and the velocity is zeroed, or damped and reversed when `mode & 4` is set. Matched with no casts.

- Crossing condition: `(before <= 0 && after > 0) || (before >= 0 && after < 0)`. The disjunct order is byte-required, because the target tests `before > 0` first. The parked draft's `(before <= 0 || after < 0) && (before >= 0 || after > 0)` was also semantically wrong: it is true when before == after == 0.
- Byte-required duplicated assignment: `position = group->unk10;` at the end of both bounce arms, after the velocity update. Cross-jumping merges the identical tails after reload into the join copy the target has (`add r0, r1, #0`, then `mov r1, ip` for the axis test). Every single-site spelling stays at 7 rows:
  - `group->unk10` directly in the stores: gcse inserts a copy right after the compare load, local-alloc ties it into r1, and the axis-flag reload takes r0.
  - `position = group->unk10;` once at the join: cse2 follows the jumps and propagates the older gcse register into both stores.
  - A `limit` user variable (22–23 rows, and no declaration order helps), `position -= before` (49), advancing `position` in place (50), ternary or default-then-if bounce forms, and velocity-first stores.
  - Assigning `position` at the *start* of both bounce arms breaks (58), because it is then live across the multiply's r0 temporaries.
  A grid over velocity variable × position source × bounce form × store order confirmed 7 as the floor for all single-site forms.
- Byte-required locals: `mode` (unk16, loaded once before the loop), `axisMask`, `horizontalAxis` (precomputed masks), and `before`.
- Folded, byte-neutral: the `mode & 4` local; `(mode & 4) != 0` is tested directly. Its width was also irrelevant (unk16 vs unk32).
- Useful diagnostics for this class of residual: `.greg` conflicts showed the gcse copy (pseudo 76) conflicting with the multiply temporaries, and `-fno-gcse` in a scratch compile showed the join reload becoming a real `ldr`. That localized the difference to the gcse, cse2 and cross-jump interaction rather than types.

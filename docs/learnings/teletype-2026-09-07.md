# Teletype

## sub_8063F64 (0x8063F64)

- The function stores zero at offset `0`, passes the embedded `SpriteTextBlock` at offset `0xEC` to `sub_8060CDC`, then loads the `AllocatedBlock *` at offset `0xC4` and calls `deallocateBlock`.
- `TeletypeState` fields `unk0`, `unkC4`, and embedded `unkEC` are required to reproduce the target address formation and access widths.

## sub_806415C (0x806415C)

- `unkBA` is accessed with `ldrh` and `strh`; the matching source applies `(unkBA & 0xFFFB) | 0x10` after calling `sub_8063CF4`.
- A local prototype for `sub_8063CF4(TeletypeState *)` was sufficient to reproduce the call and literal-pool mask exactly.

## sub_806417C (0x806417C)

- `unkBA` is loaded as a halfword, shifted right by two, and masked with one; returning `(state->unkBA >> 2) & 1` as `unk8` reproduces all six target instructions.

## sub_8064188 (0x8064188)

- `unkBA` is loaded as a halfword, shifted right by one, and masked with one; returning `(state->unkBA >> 1) & 1` as `unk8` reproduces all six target instructions.

## sub_8063F5C (0x8063F5C)

- The function stores its callback parameter at offset `0xDC`; declaring `unkDC` as a function-pointer field reproduces the direct word store without a cast.

## Bitfield experiment for `unkBA`

- A halfword allocation unit with named one-bit fields `unkBA_0` through `unkBA_4` and an unnamed eleven-bit remainder was tested at all three sites.
- In `sub_806415C`, the first divergence was at `+0x0A`: the target uses `ldr` for the halfword mask, while the bitfield form emits `mov #5`; it then emits `negs`, `ldrb`, and a final `strb` instead of the target `ldrh` and `strh`.
- In `sub_806417C`, the first divergence was at `+0x02`: the target uses `ldrh`, while the bitfield form emits `ldrb`; the bitfield form then emits `lsl #29` and `lsr #31` instead of the target `lsr #2`.
- In `sub_8064188`, the first divergence was at `+0x02`: the target uses `ldrh`, while the bitfield form emits `ldrb`; the bitfield form then emits `lsl #30` and `lsr #31` instead of the target `lsr #1`.
- The scalar `unk16 unkBA` form is retained because named bitfield accesses do not reproduce the target whole-halfword reads and read-modify-write.

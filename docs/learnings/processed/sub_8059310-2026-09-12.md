# sub_8059310

Matched at 0x08059310 in src/layer.c. The routine clips horizontal map-copy
requests and either wraps or clears the remainder. Existing BGLayer fields
and callback declarations suffice; no header changes or rule-breaking shapes.
Signed x, widthValue, endX and remainder comparisons are proven by BLE/BGE.
Existing signed coordinate callers were inspected in sub_8058968,
sub_8058A28 and sub_80596AC. mainLoop reachability was confirmed.

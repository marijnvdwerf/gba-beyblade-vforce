## newLayerManagement (0x080506F4)

The function remains parked after the coordinator's eight-build cap. The dump at `asm/dump/804a388-tutorial/80506f4-newLayerManagement.s` is retained. The best near-match has the target's 40-byte frame, saved-register mask, call sequence, loop structure, palette calls, and output-store order, but it retains a register-allocation difference.

The first structural difference is therefore the `0x52` branch displacement, while the first meaningful allocation difference after the lifetime adjustment is `0xA0`: target `mov r6, #64`, near-match `mov r7, #64`. The same swapped roles continue through the layer-mode value and later output stores. No further matching attempts were made after the cap.

`FrontendState.unkB8` and the following `unk140`/`unk1C8`/`unk250` fields are declared `FrontendSubobject`, a distinct 0x88-byte layout from `BGLayer` even though the four slots provide the storage walked by the layer-record code. Therefore the public `newLayerManagement` prototype and all five callers retain the natural `unk32*` pointer. The parked draft uses a typed `DisplayRecord*` parameter only inside its `#if 0` scratch view, where its proven fixed-stride accesses are documented without introducing caller casts.

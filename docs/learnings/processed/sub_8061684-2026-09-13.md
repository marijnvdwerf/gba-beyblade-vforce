- Folding the staged child assignment into `if (text->ptr2C != NULL)` was tested and failed at offset `0x4E`: the target loads the existing child into `r4`, while the folded form loads into `r0` and then moves it to `r4`. The staged assignment is retained.
- Hoisting the duplicated non-null-child update tail was tested. The first divergence moved to offset `0x1C` (`bne` in the target versus `beq` into the newly shared acquisition join), and the candidate was 0x1A bytes shorter. The target's duplicated tails are therefore required by the natural source CFG.

It first diverged at offset `0x9E` with `asr #5`, `mov #31`, and `lsl #25` instead of the target's full-word mask sequence `mov #0xF8; lsl #2; and; lsl #20`; the newly-created-child copy first diverged at `0x122` in the same way. `SpriteEntry.x` is also used as a full fixed-point coordinate, so no bitfield or overlay was introduced.
- The target's `sprite->unk10` update is a full-word read-modify-write (`ldr/and/orr/str`) at offsets `0xF0`–`0xF6` and `0x174`–`0x17A`. Existing SpriteEntry users likewise read and update `unk10` as a scalar word, so a bitfield representation would require a prohibited overlay and was not added.

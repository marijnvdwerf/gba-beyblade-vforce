## Source shape (measured)

- Every `MenuState` access goes through `state = &object->menuState` (r9),
  except the early `objectCount == 0` test, which the target computes from the
  object pointer (`add r0, r7, #0x490`). Writing the other accesses through
  `object->menuState` loads them via separate `object + 0x4xx` literals.
- Byte-required: passing `(sign > 0) ? -0x10 : 0xF0` directly to
  `sub_8061844` (s16 prototype). A separate `s32 direction` local adds an
  s32→s16 conversion pseudo in the loop; that lengthens `motion`'s live range
  (9 refs / 75 insns vs `object` 9 refs / 74 insns), so global-alloc gives
  `object` r6 and `motion` r7, swapping the target's r7/r6 roles and adding a
  `mov r1, r10`. An `s16 direction` local is worse (pool literal + `lsl/asr`).
- Byte-required: `unk32 language = getLanguage();` staged before the call.
  Passing `getLanguage()` directly moves the `lsl #24; lsr #24` narrowing up
  to right after the `bl`.
- Byte-required: `table = &_8068840;` loaded before the `data != NULL` test
  (kept in r8); direct `_8068840.field` access gives 84 differing rows.

# Math matching notes

## sub_805A00C (0x0805A00C)

- Signature draft: `s32* sub_805A00C(s32 a, s32 b, s32 c, s32* out)`.
- The angle inputs are normalized with arithmetic right shift by one and a byte conversion before table indexing. The table values are loaded with signed halfword loads, so the draft uses `s16` table-value locals. The products and output elements remain `s32`.
- The natural quaternion/trigonometric draft is retained in `#if 0` above the asm include. The first tested divergence was at offset `0x0A`; subsequent instructions were therefore not used as evidence for layout or signedness.
- Interleaving angle normalization with cosine and sine table loads reduced the diff versus grouped lookup forms. Product temporaries for `cosB * cosC` and `sinB * sinC` were retained because folding either changed the first divergence. Widening the table-value locals changed the generated loads and worsened the diff. Pointer, return-spelling, and output-alias variants did not remove the first divergence.
- Status: parked; the dump remains authoritative.

## sub_805A2DC (0x0805A2DC)

- Matched signature: `void sub_805A2DC(s32 (*a)[3], s32 (*b)[3], s32 (*out)[3])`.
- The function is expressed as a 3x3 matrix product with `out[i][j] = (a[i][0] * b[0][j] + a[i][1] * b[1][j] + a[i][2] * b[2][j]) >> 8` in nested `unk16` loops. The generated accesses are word loads/stores and the accumulation ends with `asr #8`.
- The direct flat-pointer formula and row-pointer variants were natural but changed loop/register lifetimes. The typed `s32 (*)[3]` matrix parameters and direct `a[i][k] * b[k][j]` expression reproduce the expected instruction stream exactly without raw-offset casts or register-step temporaries.
- The earlier byte-address integer-cast form also matched, but was discarded as an artificial raw-offset matching shape.
- The compiler emitted a trailing `0xC046` alignment nop after the function; `ASM_ZEROPAD` was required to reproduce the dump's zero alignment and restore the full ROM hash.
- Status: matched; the asm dump was removed after the exact per-function diff and full ROM compare.
